# logo-titulo

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 905
- Tamaño: 1792x768

## Estilo

Old engraving style: fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey, paper grain. Soft light from the left.

## Cámara

Game title logo, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Logo: the words "SALTO ATRÁS" in large elegant engraved serif capital letters with fine hatching inside the letters, a thin engraved rule above and below, a tiny old car silhouette under the title. The only text is exactly SALTO ATRÁS, spelled correctly.
