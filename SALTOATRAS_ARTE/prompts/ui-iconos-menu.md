# ui-iconos-menu

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 918
- Tamaño: 1792x1152

## Estilo

Old engraving style: fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey, paper grain. Soft light from the left.

## Cámara

Game interface asset sheet: separate small icons arranged in a neat grid with wide empty space between them, each seen perfectly straight from the front, flat, no perspective, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Objects: small engraved menu icons, each inside a round medallion: a case folder (dossier), a folded paper map, a magnifying glass, a cork board with pins, a pair of speaking lips (talk), an eye (look), a gear (settings), an open door (exit). No text, no letters.
