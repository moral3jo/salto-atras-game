# panel-corcho

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 805
- Tamaño: 2304x1296

## Estilo

Illustration halfway between a photograph and an old engraving: realistic forms rendered with fine etched ink hatching, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey shadows, paper grain. Soft light from the left. Stylized, not hyperrealistic.

## Cámara

Full-bleed image filling the whole frame edge to edge, seen perfectly straight from the front, no perspective.

## Escena

Scene: an empty office cork board filling the whole picture, a few small holes from old pins, a couple of empty coloured pushpins stuck in the margins, a thin worn wooden frame visible only at the very edges. Nothing pinned on it, no papers, no text.
