## Estilo

Old engraving style: fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey, paper grain. Soft light from the left.

## Cámara

Game title logo, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Logo: the words "SALTO ATRÁS" hand-written in confident blue ballpoint pen capitals on a strip of torn old paper, the second word slightly lower, underlined twice. The only text is exactly SALTO ATRÁS, spelled correctly.
