# logo-emblema

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 904
- Tamaño: 1344x1024

## Estilo

Old engraving style: fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey, paper grain. Soft light from the left.

## Cámara

Game logo emblem: one single round engraved vignette, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Emblem: inside an oval engraved frame, a lonely road at sunset leading to a small Spanish hill village with a bell gable, a small old car seen from behind driving towards it, a dark pine forest to one side, long shadows. No text, no letters.
