## Estilo

## Cámara

Game title logo, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Logo: the words "SALTO ATRÁS" in bold condensed engraved capital letters like an old film title card, letters filled with fine horizontal hatching, a thin double frame around, a tiny film-reel ornament at each side. The only text is exactly SALTO ATRÁS, spelled correctly.
