# ui-bocadillo

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 916
- Tamaño: 1792x1024

## Estilo

Old engraving style: fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey, paper grain. Soft light from the left.

## Cámara

Game interface asset: one single flat object seen perfectly straight from the front, no perspective, filling most of the picture, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Object: an empty speech balloon for a comic-style dialogue, wide and rounded, drawn with a slightly wobbly engraved ink outline and a short tail at the lower left, the inside plain cream paper with very light hatching at the edges, completely empty. No text, no letters.
