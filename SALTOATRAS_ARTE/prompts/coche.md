## Estilo

Illustration halfway between a photograph and an old engraving: realistic forms rendered with fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching, like a 19th-century steel engraving printed over a faded photograph, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey shadows, paper grain. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, long shadows falling to the right, warm golden light on surfaces facing left. Stylized, not hyperrealistic. No text, no letters, no signs with writing.

## Cámara

Game object art for a point-and-click adventure game: one single object, whole and centred with space around, seen from eye level of a standing adult with a normal 35mm lens, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background with no floor, no shadow on the background, nothing else.

## Escena

Object: a modest, slightly dusty old compact car from the 1990s, faded dark red, a small dent on the door, seen in three-quarter view from the front left, wheels turned, as if parked carelessly at an angle. Blank number plates with no letters, no logos.
