## Estilo

Illustration halfway between a photograph and an old engraving: realistic forms rendered with fine etched ink hatching, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey shadows, paper grain. Soft light from the left. Stylized, not hyperrealistic.

## Cámara

Game interface asset: one single flat object seen perfectly straight from the front, no perspective, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background, nothing else.

## Escena

Object: a plain sheet of white paper pinned with a single drawing pin, as a notice put up on the town hall door, slightly curled at the bottom corners, a faint fold mark across the middle. Completely blank: absolutely no writing, no text.
