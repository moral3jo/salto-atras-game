## Estilo

Illustration halfway between a photograph and an old engraving: realistic forms rendered with fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching, like a 19th-century steel engraving printed over a faded photograph, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey shadows, paper grain. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, warm golden light on surfaces facing left, cool shadows on the right. Stylized, not hyperrealistic. No text, no letters.

## Cámara

Character art for a point-and-click adventure game: one single character, full body from head to shoes, standing, three-quarter view facing slightly to the left, eye-level camera with a normal 50mm lens, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background with no floor, no shadow on the background, nothing else.

## Escena

Make him just a little older, about 14 or 15: a young teenager halfway between a child and an adult, a bit taller and thinner with slightly longer legs, his face still smooth and boyish with no facial hair, only slightly less round. Healthy warm lightly tanned skin with natural colour in the cheeks, not pale.
