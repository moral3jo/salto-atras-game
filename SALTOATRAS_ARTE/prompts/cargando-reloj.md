# cargando-reloj

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 925
- Tamaño: 1024x1024

## Estilo

Old engraving style: fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey, paper grain. Soft light from the left.

## Cámara

Game interface asset: one single flat object seen perfectly straight from the front, no perspective, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background.

## Escena

Object: an old brass pocket watch seen exactly face-on, open, perfectly circular, a plain dial with only tick marks and no numbers, two hands, for a loading spinner. No text, no letters, no numbers.
