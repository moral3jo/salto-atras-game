## Estilo

Illustration halfway between a photograph and an old engraving: realistic forms rendered with fine etched ink hatching and cross-hatching, like a 19th-century steel engraving, limited warm palette of sepia, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta and cold blue-grey shadows, paper grain. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left. Stylized, not hyperrealistic. No text, no letters.

## Cámara

Small icon for a detective game dossier: the subject inside a round engraved medallion with a thin double ring border, like an old coin or a 19th-century engraved vignette, centred and filling the medallion, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background outside the circle.

## Escena

Subject: an anonymous unknown man, head-and-shoulders, three-quarter view facing left, shown as a completely black solid silhouette with no face features at all, wearing a suit jacket and tie, like a 'wanted: unknown person' placeholder; a faint warm rim of light on his left edge.
