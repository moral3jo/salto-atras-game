# plaza-d-acuarela

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 101
- Tamaño: 2304x1296 (960x540 + 10 % de margen por lado, a x2)
- Prompt = ESTILO + CÁMARA + ESCENA, separados por un espacio.

## Estilo

Ink and watercolour illustration like a European graphic novel (ligne claire meets Pentiment): confident dark ink linework, soft transparent watercolour washes, visible paper texture, warm earthy palette of ochre, terracotta, olive and faded blue. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, long shadows falling to the right, warm golden light on surfaces facing left. No text, no letters, no signs with writing.

## Cámara

Full-bleed image filling the whole frame edge to edge, no border, no vignette. Camera: first-person fixed screen of a point-and-click adventure game, eye level of a standing adult, looking straight at the scene from the front with a normal 35mm lens; the main building faces the camera, parallel to the picture plane, in the centre; open paved ground across the lower third. Important things are kept inside the central area, with extra calm scenery at all four edges.

## Escena

Scene: the main square of a small, half-empty inland Spanish village. In the centre, the town hall: a two-storey old stone building with an arcade of round arches at ground level, a wooden balcony and a small bell gable on top, closed wooden door. Around the square, modest whitewashed and stone houses with closed shutters, a small shop with a rolled-down metal shutter, a couple of bare plane trees, a stone bench and an old stone fountain on the right. On a wall, an empty green wall cabinet with its glass door open (a removed defibrillator). On the far right, a narrow street leading away between houses. Lower left, empty paved space. No people, no cars, quiet and slightly melancholic.
