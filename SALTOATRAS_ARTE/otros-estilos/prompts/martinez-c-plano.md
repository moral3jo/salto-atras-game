# martinez-c-plano

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 202
- Tamaño: 896x1344
- Prompt = ESTILO + CÁMARA + ESCENA, separados por un espacio.

## Estilo

Minimalist theatrical illustration in the style of Kentucky Route Zero: flat geometric shapes, clean silhouettes, large areas of flat colour, very few details, faces simplified, strong graphic shapes, quiet magical-realist mood, muted palette of dusty ochre, faded teal and deep indigo shadows. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, hard-edged shadow shapes on the right side of the figure. No text, no letters.

## Cámara

Character art for a point-and-click adventure game: one single character, full body from head to shoes, standing, three-quarter view facing slightly to the right, eye-level camera with a normal 50mm lens, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background with no floor, no shadow on the background, nothing else.

## Escena

Character: Javier Martínez, a ministry civil servant in his early forties, average build, tired ironic half-smile, short dark hair, light stubble, rumpled grey suit with a loosened tie, holding a worn leather briefcase, wearing a slightly ridiculous knitted wool beanie with a pompom.
