# martinez-detective-d-acuarela

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 202
- Tamaño: 896x1344
- Prompt = ESTILO + CÁMARA + ESCENA, separados por un espacio.

## Estilo

Ink and watercolour illustration like a European graphic novel (ligne claire meets Pentiment): confident dark ink linework, soft transparent watercolour washes, visible paper texture, warm earthy palette of ochre, terracotta, olive and faded blue. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, warm golden light on surfaces facing left, cool shadows on the right. No text, no letters.

## Cámara

Character art for a point-and-click adventure game: one single character, full body from head to shoes, standing, three-quarter view facing slightly to the right, eye-level camera with a normal 50mm lens, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background with no floor, no shadow on the background, nothing else.

## Escena

Character: Javier Martínez, a ministry civil servant in his early forties who dresses like a 1980s private investigator: a brown felt fedora hat, a beige trench coat open over a rumpled grey suit and loosened tie, holding a worn leather briefcase. Average build, tired ironic half-smile, short dark hair, light stubble.
