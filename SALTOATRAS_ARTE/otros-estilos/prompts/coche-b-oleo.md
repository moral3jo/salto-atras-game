# coche-b-oleo

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 404
- Tamaño: 1344x896
- Prompt = ESTILO + CÁMARA + ESCENA, separados por un espacio.

## Estilo

Painterly oil painting in the style of Disco Elysium's backgrounds: loose expressive brush strokes, thick paint, muted desaturated colours with a few warm accents, realistic proportions but stylized and moody, slightly melancholic. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, warm golden light on surfaces facing left, cool shadows on the right. No text, no letters.

## Cámara

Game object art for a point-and-click adventure game: one single object, whole and centred with space around, seen from eye level of a standing adult with a normal 35mm lens, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background with no floor, no shadow on the background, nothing else.

## Escena

Object: a modest, slightly dusty old compact car from the 1990s, faded dark red, a small dent on the door, seen in three-quarter view from the front left, wheels turned, as if parked carelessly at an angle. Blank number plates with no letters, no logos.
