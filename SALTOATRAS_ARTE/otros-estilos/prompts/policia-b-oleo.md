# policia-b-oleo

- Workflow: ComfyUI `GameAssets/Fondos (Klein 4B)` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, 4 pasos)
- Semilla: 303
- Tamaño: 896x1344
- Prompt = ESTILO + CÁMARA + ESCENA, separados por un espacio.

## Estilo

Painterly oil painting in the style of Disco Elysium's character portraits: loose expressive brush strokes, thick paint, muted desaturated colours with a few warm accents, realistic proportions but stylized and moody, slightly melancholic. Single light source: low late-afternoon sun from the left, warm golden light on surfaces facing left, cool shadows on the right. No text, no letters.

## Cámara

Character art for a point-and-click adventure game: one single character, full body from head to shoes, standing, three-quarter view facing slightly to the left, eye-level camera with a normal 50mm lens, centred with space around, on a perfectly plain flat light grey background with no floor, no shadow on the background, nothing else.

## Escena

Character: a Spanish village local policeman in his mid fifties, stocky and tired, hard unfriendly face, grey moustache, heavy eyelids, in a plain dark navy local police uniform with a peaked cap with a black-and-white checkered band, dark trousers, black boots, holding a small ticket booklet and a pen as if about to write a parking fine. No badges with text.
